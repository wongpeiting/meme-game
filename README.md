# Peak Meme

An interactive game where you play as a social media editor at the White House TikTok account. Real events from August 2025 to March 2026 land on your desk as raw footage. You decide how to package each one.

Your boss wants views. The algorithm has preferences. Every choice you make will be measured.

**Play it here:** [wongpeiting.github.io/meme-game](https://wongpeiting.github.io/meme-game/)

## About

Built as a companion piece to [How the White House Hit Peak Meme](https://wongpeiting.github.io/peak-meme/), a scrollytelling investigation into the White House TikTok account's 600 posts and 1.45 billion views.

All view counts, captions, and post descriptions referenced in this game are from real data.

## How it works

- 8 rounds following the real timeline
- Each round presents raw footage and asks how you'd package it
- Choices range from Level 1 (official press briefing) to Level 7 (game UI on real footage)
- The endgame compares your choices against what the real White House actually did
- Includes a video reel of the 12 posts where the White House packaged real combat footage as internet entertainment

## Built with

- [Ink](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) (interactive fiction scripting language)
- [inkjs](https://github.com/y-lohse/inkjs) (JavaScript Ink runtime)
- Custom HTML/CSS/JS web player

## Data

Based on 600 White House TikTok posts, collected and classified August 2025 to April 2026.
